About Me

My name is Joshua I am a 15 year old student who loves to learn and create. I am currently unemployed, but am looking for a job. if you wish you may contact me through my email ynotsusworkspace@gmail.com.

I first discovered engineering through someone who most of you know, Mark Rober. Specifically his video "Snowball Machine Gun- How to make," and thought "Hey that would be cool." I didn't start making anything until i was 13. I had made a small alarm thing. What would happen is that if it would tilt, a small ball bearing would contact two metal probes sticking into the syringe the ball was in. Then it would complete the circuit and a small LED light would turn on. My dad thought it was pretty cool and so did I.

My next project, or should I say projects, were Mark Rober's Crunchlabs boxes I was a little to old for the but still enjoyed it. I remember modifying the Disc Launcher using a 9V battery an replacing the old 1.5 volt batteries. While it did shoot faster I soon lost my most of my discs.

When the boxes stopped coming I got bored, so for my birthday my parents got me HackPack. These were much more fun than the Crunchlabs boxes. Now there were circuits and Arudinos inside. My favorite then and now is the self stabalising 2-wheeled robot. I entered it into my school's competition and got 2nd, which is not saying much when there were only 2 people who entered the category I did.

Now I am working on a project to enter this year,and currently learning coding. I hope to one day he able to be able to code things like Stuff Made Here, make things like Hacksmith Industries, and to hack like Network Chuck. My goal for the future is to make a TRUE superhero suit that is extremely versatile and does not limit my bodies movement.
